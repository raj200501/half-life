## 1. Questions and hypotheses

Directions are not presumed: rules that persist is a valid finding. Every test is two-sided at alpha 0.05.

- **H1 (RQ1, position).** In each family, the per-opportunity violation hazard changes with `log1p(position)`, where position is the tool-call index at which a decision point starts, counted from the start of the session (the rules are stated before the first call), adjusted for the compaction history and the rule type. Positions are randomized through the plans' filler counts, but the tool-call index also counts the calls the agent chose to make, so H1 is an adjusted association, not a randomized effect.
- **H2b (RQ2, compaction count).** In each family, the hazard changes with `compaction_count`, the number of compactions at or before the decision point's start, adjusted for position, rule type, and compaction arm. The count includes scheduled compactions, whose timing is randomized, and compactions the token budget triggers, whose timing depends on the session's own token use, so H2b is an adjusted association, not the causal effect of a compaction.
- **H2a (RQ2, losing the rule text; the randomized test).** In each family and pooled over the two families, among opportunities with at least one compaction before them, the violation rate differs between the `full_summary` arm and the `sham` arm. The arm is assigned to plans by blocked randomization. The sham arm runs the same summary call but keeps the whole opening user message (the rules and the first request) verbatim, so the contrast isolates losing that message's text from the rest of a compaction.

**Confirmatory tests:** H1 and H2b in each family, and H2a in each family and pooled: six hypotheses, each decided at alpha 0.05, with no adjustment across them. **Preregistered estimates** (with intervals, no test): the hazard coefficients of both models per family and pooled, and the half-life per family. **Exploratory**, computed and reported with their p-values but deciding nothing: pooled H1 and pooled H2b (their variance cannot represent a decision-point effect the families share, D-060), and the rule-type contrasts, H3 (D-064: rule types are nested in templates, and nothing calibrates a test of them).

## 2. Design

- **Agents.** The minimal agent (`halflife.agent`) with its fixed scaffold on two local open-weight families, chosen by the exploratory pilot before any E1 data (`artifacts/pilot/family_choice.json`): `smollm2-1.7b-instruct-q4km` (SHA-256 `77665ea4815999596525c636fbeb56ba8b080b46ae85efef4f0d986a139834d7`) and `granite-3.3-2b-instruct-q4km` (SHA-256 `555b91485955bc96eb445b57dd4bbf8809aa7d8cce7c313f4f8bc5b2340896b4`), pinned in `artifacts/models/pins.json`, run through llama.cpp on CPU with a context of 8192 tokens, temperature 0.7, and at most 384 tokens per step (the `halflife.experiment run` defaults, pinned in section 10).
- **Templates.** The 21 templates of the `main` split of half-life-bench (`artifacts/bench/manifest.json`). The pilot and held-out templates are not used.
- **Plans.** `designs/e1.yaml`: 48 plans, which every family runs, each with three to five decision points. Templates and the two compaction arms are assigned by blocked randomization; decision-point positions come from random filler counts; one or two scheduled compactions per session fall at random tool-call indices in [8, 70), at least 15 calls apart, drawn independently of positions, and a 3,000-token budget compacts whenever the context grows past it (D-043, D-047). Sessions are padded to at least 20 requests and capped at 150 tool calls and 12 calls per request, with a recall probe before every decision point and after every compaction: forked contexts whose text never enters the main run (probes do draw from the session's seed sequence, so the main run's sampled tokens depend on how many probes ran, alike in both arms).
- **Order.** `tools/round_robin.py --plans runs/e1/plans.jsonl --prefix e1 --models smollm2-1.7b-instruct-q4km granite-3.3-2b-instruct-q4km --prereg prereg/0001-e1.md` runs plan index i on both families before index i+1, so slow periods on the machine hit both alike. Reserve plans, if the reach rule calls for them, run the same way from `runs/e1/reserve-plans.jsonl` with the prefix `e1r`, one block of eight indices at a time (`--indices 0 1 2 3 4 5 6 7`, then 8 to 15).

## 3. Outcomes

- **Violation** at an opportunity: the deterministic detectors validated in E0 (`artifacts/e0/detector_validation.json`) flag a violation of the decision point's rule inside its span. E0 validated the detectors on seeded action logs; `src/halflife/detect.py` has not changed since, and how the current sandbox (D-025) records actions is exercised by the compliant routes and reference runs under `artifacts/bench/`, not by E0.
- **Exploratory:** recall before the decision point (the E0-validated scorer, grouped by the compactions the probe saw), task success, tokens, and wall time.

## 4. Analysis set and exclusions

Each is counted and reported, in total and per family.

1. Sessions: confirmatory rows tagged with this preregistration, without a probe leak, that reached their first decision point.
2. Opportunities of kept sessions whose decision-point span has at least one tool call.
3. Procedure-rule opportunities whose span ended by a call cap instead of the agent finishing (`span_end` other than `done`), which cannot show whether the procedure was followed (D-050 item 5).

The hazard models (H1, H2b, the half-life) and the Kaplan-Meier curves use each session's risk set: its opportunities, in order, up to and including its first violation. H2a and the exploratory rate contrasts use every opportunity of the analysis set (H2a those after at least one compaction).

## 5. Analysis

`python -m halflife.e1_analysis --dir artifacts/e1 --n-boot 2000 --seed 20261104` at the pinned code (section 10).

- **Hazard models.** Logistic regressions per opportunity on the risk set, per family, and pooled with a family factor for the descriptive intervals (D-059 to D-061). H1's model has the covariates `log1p(position)` (the tested term) and `compaction_count`, and the factors rule type and compaction cell (the compaction arm crossed with the compaction level 0, 1, or 2 or more), so a step in the hazard at a compaction, in either arm, or growth past two compactions is not read as a position effect. H2b's model has the covariates `log1p(position)` and `compaction_count` (the tested term), and the factors rule type, compaction arm, and position band (bands of ten tool calls below 70, `[0,10)` to `[60,70)`, and `[70,150]`), so a step in the hazard by position is not read as a compaction effect.
- **Decisions.** Each hypothesis is decided by a two-sided score test of its term at the null fit of the model without it, at alpha 0.05, clustered by plan through an exchangeable within-plan correlation estimated at the null fit (rows whose fitted null probability is exactly 0 or 1 add nothing and leave that estimate, D-063); pooled, the null model is fit within each family and the score sums over families (D-049, D-050, D-058). Supported means p below 0.05; the direction is the sign of the score. H2a tests the arm term among opportunities after at least one compaction.
- **Not tested.** H1 or H2b is not tested in a family when its fit holds fewer than 7 violations (`few_violations`), the minimum set before any E1 data by a synthetic calibration of these tests on the E1 plans (`artifacts/power/min_events.json`; D-050 item 3, D-061, D-063): from that count up, every per-family cell of 15 null scenarios kept its null rejection rate between 3% and 7%. Any hypothesis is not tested when no valid p-value exists, with the reason the analysis records (for example `null_separation`, `rank_deficient`, `single_value`, or `one_cluster`), and H2a in a family whose opportunities after a compaction lack one of the arms (`arm_absent`). Not tested is reported as such, never as support or its absence.
- **Descriptive estimates.** Coefficients with percentile bootstrap intervals from 2,000 resamples of whole plans (seed 20261104); an interval with more than one draw in ten failing is labeled unstable, and a term without a finite estimate is reported as not estimable with its reason. Neither changes a decision.
- **Half-life.** Per family, from a hazard model with `log1p(position)` only (marginal over compaction), the position at which the predicted hazard reaches one half, searched from the family's first observed decision point to 150; reported as not reached within 150 tool calls, at or before a position, or with an open upper bound when too many bootstrap draws never reach one half (D-028, D-049, D-050 item 4). A separated fit gives no half-life (not estimable). It is read within the observed range (D-064): a half-life past the family's largest observed decision-point position is reported as an extrapolation, and a half-life not reached is also reported as not reached within that largest position.
- **Kaplan-Meier** curves of time to first violation are descriptive only.

## 6. Sample size and stopping

- **48 plans per family** (96 sessions), set by compute: one E1-shaped session took about 23 to 46 minutes on this machine in dry runs, so E1 takes several days of CPU. The power analysis `artifacts/power/e1.json` (synthetic; docs/REPORT.md section 5.4) approximates the H2b and H2a tests with simpler models on a generic session shape (D-063) and shows low power for small and moderate effects at every size the compute allows, so a hypothesis that is not supported is reported as inconclusive, not as evidence that the effect is absent.
- **Reach rule.** G7 needs at least 40 sessions per family that reached their first decision point. If a family has fewer after the 48 plans, the plans of `designs/e1-reserve.yaml` run in index order on both families, in blocks of 8, until both families have 40 or all 16 have run. The rule looks only at reach, never at violations, and G7 checks it (D-062).
- **No interim analysis.** No violation outcome is analyzed before every planned session has run. Finished sessions' raw outputs are checkpointed as they finish (`tools/checkpoint_runs.py` into `artifacts/e1/raw/`), unanalyzed, so that a lost machine loses no session; the E1 files are banked once, after the last planned session (`python -m halflife.experiment bank --experiment e1 --runs runs/e1-<model> ... --out artifacts/e1 --prereg prereg/0001-e1.md --design designs/e1.yaml`, adding the `runs/e1r-<model>` runs when reserve plans ran); each run's record names its plans file and that file's SHA-256, which must equal a plans hash above.
- **Stopping for cause only:** a usage-cap stop, a harness defect (recorded in DECISIONS.md and reported as a deviation), or the disk floor. A session that stops partway is rerun from its start by the resumable runner; a finished session is never rerun. Sessions already run are kept and reported.

## 8. Limitations stated in advance

- H1 and H2b are adjusted associations (section 1). RQ2's causal test is H2a, and even there the condition "at least one compaction before the decision point" includes budget compactions, whose timing can differ between arms (the sham arm keeps more text), so the arms are compared within a set that the arm can shape.
- A state rule the session already broke cannot show a second violation at a later decision point, which pulls later violation rates toward zero for the rate contrasts; the hazard models stop at the first violation and are not affected.
- Dropping procedure spans that a call cap cut (section 4, item 3) selects on how a span ended, which may relate to compliance.
- H2a's test is conservative when rates differ in ways its null model does not represent (D-060), a cost in power.
- The calibration covers 15 synthetic null scenarios on these plans and nothing else; its margin is thin, since the highest per-family null rejection rate at or above the minimum is just under 7% (an H2b cell; `deciding_extremes` in the artifact), so H2b's size can reach the band's upper edge under some nulls.
- The subjects are two small quantized open-weight models on a CPU; nothing here generalizes to frontier models without E2 or later studies.

## 9. Exploratory (not tested here)

H3 and per-rule-type rates and half-lives (RQ4; RQ1 asks for half-lives per rule type, and too few violations per type are expected for a confirmatory estimate), recall analyses, task success, per-template and per-position descriptive rates, prior-violation effects, a hazard model counting only scheduled compactions, and the Kaplan-Meier curves.
